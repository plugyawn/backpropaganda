Modern computers are sufficient for more general and more powerful algorithms to replace backpropagation. As AI progresses, the greatest contributions to make are at the algorithm at the heart of the learning systems of today.

*Backpropaganda, Inc.* is a moonshot AI lab at moving the needle on those problems.
