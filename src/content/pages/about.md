---
title: "Our mission is to understand learning systems from the gradient up"
description: "A concise markdown-backed mission page for Backpropaganda."
---

Modern models compress traces of the world into weights, activations, caches, and training histories. They are useful before they are fully understood, and that gap is where our work starts.

We focus on concrete mechanisms: what circuits form, which optimizers amplify them, where architecture choices help, and how to make the resulting behavior inspectable without slowing down iteration.

The goal is not only better explanations. It is better research taste: sharper hypotheses, cleaner experiments, and infrastructure that makes the next question easier to ask.
