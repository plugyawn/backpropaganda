A Telescope, Pointed at the Stars

A short announcement about building instruments for model internals rather than treating interpretability as an afterthought.

ANNOUNCEMENT 08.10.25

Good instruments change the questions a lab can ask. They make weak signals visible, standardize comparisons, and reduce the cost of being wrong.

Backpropaganda starts from that premise: better model understanding needs better tools, but those tools should stay close to the research loop they are meant to accelerate.
